**2. Purpose and target audience**
Planificador de ruta camión is a free trip-planning tool for professional truck (heavy goods vehicle) drivers, dispatchers and small transport companies working on European roads.

It turns an origin and a destination into a complete trip plan:
- the road route and distance;
- the mandatory driving breaks and daily rests under EU Regulation (EC) No 561/2006, with the time and kilometre of each one and the alternatives the regulation permits (split break, reduced daily rest);
- planning for one or two drivers;
- estimated tolls per country, based on the vehicle's weight, axles and EURO class;
- estimated fuel and total trip cost;
- the national rules of each country on the route (weekend driving bans, low-emission zones, winter equipment).

The problem it solves: today a driver combines a satnav, a toll calculator, a summary of the driving-time rules and mental arithmetic to know where and when they must stop, and mistakes lead to fines and unsafe driving. The app puts this into one plan. On iPhone it can also remind the driver before each mandatory break with local notifications. All results are presented as planning estimates; the app states clearly that they are not legally binding and that drivers must follow their tachograph and the rules in force.

There are no accounts, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The app is available in all 24 official EU languages.

**3. How to use the main features**
No login or sample files are needed.
1. Open the app. On the Plan tab, enter an origin and a destination (for example "Valencia, España" and "Lyon, Francia"), or tap "Usar mi ubicación" to use the current location as the origin (asks for location permission while using the app). Optionally set the departure time and open "Perfil del vehículo" to change weight, axles, EURO class, fuel price or the number of drivers.
2. Tap "Calcular ruta". The Resultado tab shows distance, total trip time, arrival, tolls, fuel and total cost, with tolls by country further down.
3. The Mapa tab shows the route with the planned stops.
4. The Normas tab shows the mandatory breaks and rests. "Avisarme de las pausas" schedules local notifications before each break (asks for notification permission). Below that are the national rules for each country on the route.
5. The language selector in the top bar switches between the 24 EU languages. The Info tab has the privacy policy and data sources.

**4. External services used**
- OSRM (router.project-osrm.org): road routing.
- OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): turning addresses into coordinates and the current location into an address.
- OpenStreetMap tiles (tile.openstreetmap.org) and the Leaflet map library (cdnjs.cloudflare.com): the map.
- Google Analytics 4 and an anonymous statistics endpoint on Google Cloud: aggregate usage statistics, only if the user agrees in the in-app privacy prompt.
- Tolls, national rules and the driving-time rules are built into the app as datasets; no external provider is needed for them.
- On the device: iOS location services (only for "Usar mi ubicación") and iOS local notifications (break reminders). No authentication, payment or AI services are used.

**5. Regional differences**
The app works the same in every region. Routing and address search cover the whole world. The regulation content is European: it applies the EU driving-time rules, has toll estimates for 44 European countries and national rules for 30 European countries. The app opens in Spanish, and the user can choose any of the 24 EU languages from the menu.

**6. Regulated industry and third-party material**
The app does not provide a regulated service. It is an informational planning aid: it does not operate transport, record driving time or replace a tachograph, and it says so on every result. The driving-time rules come from public EU legislation (Regulation (EC) No 561/2006). Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, used under the Open Database License with the attribution shown on the map; routing uses OSRM on OpenStreetMap data. No licences or credentials are required to provide this information.
