2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Planificador de ruta camión is a free trip-planning tool for professional truck (heavy goods vehicle) drivers, dispatchers and small transport companies on European roads. From an origin and a destination it builds a complete plan: the road route; the mandatory driving breaks and daily rests under EU Regulation (EC) No 561/2006, with the time and kilometre of each and the permitted alternatives (split break, reduced daily rest); planning for one or two drivers; estimated tolls per country by vehicle weight, axles and EURO class; fuel and total trip cost; and the national rules of each country on the route (weekend driving bans, low-emission zones, winter equipment).
Problem solved: drivers otherwise combine a satnav, a toll calculator, a summary of the driving-time rules and mental arithmetic to know where and when they must stop; mistakes lead to fines and unsafe driving. On iPhone the app can also remind the driver before each mandatory break with local notifications. Results are presented as planning estimates, not legally binding. No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases; all 24 official EU languages.

3. HOW TO USE IT
No login or sample files are needed.
- Plan tab: enter an origin and a destination (e.g. "Madrid" and "Lyon"), or tap "Usar mi ubicación" for the current location (asks for location permission while using the app). Optional: departure time; "Perfil del vehículo" for weight, axles, EURO class, fuel price and number of drivers.
- Tap "Calcular ruta". Resultado shows distance, trip time, arrival, tolls, fuel and total cost.
- Mapa shows the route with the planned stops.
- Normas shows the mandatory breaks and rests; "Avisarme de las pausas" schedules local notifications before each break (asks for notification permission). Below are the national rules for each country on the route.
- The selector in the top bar switches between the 24 languages. Info has the privacy policy.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- OSRM (router.project-osrm.org): road routing.
- OpenStreetMap Nominatim: addresses to coordinates, and the current location to an address.
- OpenStreetMap tiles and the Leaflet library (cdnjs.cloudflare.com): the map.
- Google Analytics 4 and an anonymous statistics endpoint on Google Cloud: aggregate usage statistics, only if the user agrees in the in-app privacy prompt.
- Tolls, national rules and driving-time rules are datasets built into the app.
- On the device: iOS location (only for "Usar mi ubicación") and local notifications (break reminders).
No authentication, payment or AI services.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in every region. Routing and address search are worldwide; the regulation content is European (EU driving-time rules, toll estimates for 44 European countries, national rules for 30). It opens in Spanish and offers all 24 EU languages.

6. REGULATION AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app does not provide a regulated service: it is an informational planning aid that does not operate transport, record driving time or replace a tachograph, and says so on every result. The driving-time rules are public EU legislation (Regulation (EC) No 561/2006). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License, with the attribution shown on the map. No licences or credentials are required.
