How the times are calculated
  • Driving time = distance / average truck speed (default 70 km/h).
  • A 45-minute break after every 4h30 of driving.
  • An 11-hour daily rest after every 9 hours of driving.
  • Rest stops are suggested every 350 km, with safe parkings within 50 km.
